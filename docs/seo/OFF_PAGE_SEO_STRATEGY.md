# Off-page SEO strategy

17 November 2026. This is a plan. **No account has been created, no business details have been submitted and no third party has been contacted.** Every action below needs management approval first.

## Principles

* Earn links and mentions from real relationships, real listings and genuinely useful content.
* Never buy links, join link exchanges, use private blog networks, post comment spam, or create fake profiles or reviews. These can lead to Google manual actions and permanent loss of trust.
* Use identical name, address and phone details everywhere (see `LOCAL_SEO_STRATEGY.md`). Do not publish any listing until the address, phones and hours are confirmed.
* No outcome is guaranteed. Links and mentions support visibility over months; they do not guarantee rankings.

## Process

1. **Prepare a brand fact sheet** (confirmed name, address, phones, hours, 2-sentence description, logo, 3 genuine photos). Blocked until `MANAGEMENT_INFORMATION_REQUIRED.md` items are answered.
2. **Audit each candidate site** before submitting: is it active, indexed by Google, free of spam links, and relevant to Zimbabwe or construction? Skip any that fail.
3. **Submit manually** from a business email and keep a log (site, URL of listing, date, details used, login owner).
4. **Request, never demand,** links from genuine partners. Offer something real: a reciprocal mention only where it is honest and natural.
5. **Monitor** Search Console "Links" report monthly and record new referring domains. Disavow only genuinely harmful, unsolicited spam links and only after taking advice.

## Brand mentions

Keep the brand written consistently as "Normlee Investments". Encourage customers to mention the name, not to use keyword-stuffed anchor text. Unlinked mentions still help recognition; if a legitimate mention has no link, ask politely.

## What to avoid (explicit)

Paid links; "guaranteed backlinks" or "SEO packages" from unsolicited vendors; mass directory submissions; duplicate profiles; fake or incentivised reviews; spun articles; comment or forum spam; PBNs; anchor-text manipulation.

## Measuring progress (no numbers invented)

Record, monthly: number of referring domains (Search Console), number of confirmed directory listings, number of unlinked brand mentions found, number of genuine customer reviews. Baseline is unknown today because Search Console is not connected.
