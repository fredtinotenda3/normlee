# Keyword research and search intent: Normlee Investments

Prepared 17 November 2026.

## Read this first: what this document is and is not

**No search-volume, difficulty, competition or trend data was available to this phase.** Those figures come from tools such as Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account), Search Console (after launch) or paid tools. None was accessible, and none of those numbers have been invented or estimated. Every row below has "Search-volume data: Not available".

What was actually done:

1. **SERP observation (measured, qualitative).** On 17 Nov 2026 four web searches were run for supplier and paving terms with a Harare focus. The *wording* competitors use, the *kind* of sites that appear (supplier sites, SME directories, contractor sites, company-data aggregators) and the *content they publish* were noted. This shows how Zimbabwean sellers and buyers name things. It does not show how many people search for them.
2. **Reasoned intent mapping (judgement).** Search intent, audience and priority are the author's reasoned classification, labelled as judgement.
3. **Verified business facts.** Only products on the live price list (`lib/pricing.ts`) and services on `/services` are mapped to pages. Nothing is mapped to a product or service Normlee has not confirmed.

Limitations: four searches is a small sample, results reflect one search engine session from one location and are not rankings, and competitor claims were not verified. Competitor prices seen in results varied widely and are not reproduced because they cannot be compared like for like.

## Keyword to page mapping (one primary page per intent)

| Page | Primary intent | Do not target here (avoids cannibalisation) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Brand and broad "building materials / aggregates / paving in Zimbabwe" | Individual product terms (they belong to `/products`) |
| `/products` | All specific material terms and prices | Paving service terms, delivery logistics, how-to questions |
| `/services` | Delivery and paving installation | Material product names as the main subject |
| `/blog/<guide>` | Informational questions, one topic per guide | Transactional phrases like "buy" or "price" |
| `/about`, `/contact` | Brand, trust, contact | Product or service keywords beyond a natural mention |
| `/projects`, `/testimonials` | Not targeted (noindex until content exists) | Everything |

Rules applied in copy: one primary topic per URL; product names appear once in titles; descriptions are written for people; no repeated keyword lists; "Harare" does not appear in any title or description until the address is confirmed.
